# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and for reproducible science.
- Credit data, code and ideas that are not yours.
- Do not make medical claims about research results.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insulting comments or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Uploading subject-identifying or otherwise sensitive data.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.

Report incidents privately to the maintainers listed in `CITATION.cff`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
